Alongside branding, users, and integrations, the Account area carries three settings pages
that are easy to miss. Which of them you see depends on your role: billing and usage are
restricted, and only account admins can change early access features.

## Billing

**Account > Billing** hands off to the **Stripe billing portal**, where you manage the
subscription, update payment methods, and pull invoices. Telivy does not keep a second copy
of that information in the portal.

## Usage

**Account > Usage** shows **Monitored Usage**: the active monitored endpoints and SaaS user
accounts behind your bill, per assessment.

| Column | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Account | The assessment the row belongs to |
| Endpoints | Monitored endpoints reporting in |
| M365 users | Microsoft 365 user accounts in the connected tenant |
| M365 monitoring | Whether M365 monitoring is on for that assessment |
| Workspace users | Google Workspace user accounts in the connected domain |
| Workspace monitoring | Whether Workspace monitoring is on for that assessment |
| Last refreshed | When those counts were last recalculated |

This is the page to open before questioning an invoice. Turning Microsoft 365 or Google
Workspace monitoring off for an assessment excludes those accounts from future billed
counts, so the change shows up going forward rather than retroactively.

## Early Access

**Account > Early Access** lists features you can switch on before general release, grouped
by how finished they are: **beta**, then **alpha**, then **concept**. Toggle one on and it
applies to your agency.

Two things to know:

- Only account admins can toggle features. Everyone else sees the list read-only.
- Some features are marked sensitive and ask you to acknowledge what enabling them means
  before the toggle takes effect.

Treat concept-stage features as exactly that. They are early, and they can change or
disappear.

## Unity

If your account is linked to Unity, a **Unity** entry appears indented beneath
Integrations, for syncing that connection.
