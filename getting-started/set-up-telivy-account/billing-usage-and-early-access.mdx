The Account area has three pages that are easy to walk past. What you see depends on your
role: billing and usage are restricted, and only account admins can change early access
features.

## Billing

**Account > Billing** hands off to the **Stripe billing portal**, where you manage the
subscription, update payment methods, and pull invoices. Telivy doesn't keep a second copy
of any of that.

## Usage

**Account > Usage** shows **Monitored Usage**: the active monitored endpoints and SaaS user
accounts behind your bill, per assessment.

| Column | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Account | The assessment the row belongs to |
| Endpoints | Monitored endpoints reporting in |
| M365 users | Microsoft 365 user accounts in the connected tenant |
| M365 monitoring | Whether M365 monitoring is on for that assessment |
| Workspace users | Google Workspace user accounts in the connected domain |
| Workspace monitoring | Whether Workspace monitoring is on for that assessment |
| Last refreshed | When those counts were last recalculated |

Open this before you query an invoice. Turning Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace monitoring
off for an assessment takes those accounts out of future billed counts, so the change shows
up going forward, not retroactively.

## Early Access

**Account > Early Access** lists features you can switch on ahead of general release,
grouped by how finished they are: **beta** first, then **alpha**, then **concept**. Toggle
one on and it applies to your whole agency.

Two things to know. Only account admins can flip a toggle, so everyone else sees the list
read-only. And some features are marked sensitive, which means you acknowledge what
enabling them does before the toggle takes effect.

Concept-stage features are exactly that. They change, and sometimes they disappear.

## Unity

Accounts linked to Unity get a **Unity** entry indented under Integrations, for syncing
that connection.
