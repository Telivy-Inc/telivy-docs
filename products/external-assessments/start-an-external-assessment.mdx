---
title: Start an External Assessment
description: 'Create a domain-only scan that finishes in about ten minutes'
---

An External Assessment looks at a company from the public internet only. Nothing is
installed, so it needs no cooperation from the prospect: setup takes under a minute and
the scan typically finishes in about ten minutes.

It covers dark web exposure, external network vulnerabilities, DNS evaluation, and website
vulnerability checks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment picker">
    Click **Create Assessment** in the top right corner of the portal, then choose
    **External Scan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the company details">
    Fill in **Company Name** and **Domain**, and set **Company Status** to record whether
    this is a prospect or an existing client.

    Tick **Notify me via email when external scan completes** if you would rather not
    watch the assessment page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the assessment">
    Click **Create**. The scan starts immediately and findings appear as they land.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Scanning many prospects at once? Upload a CSV instead of creating them one by one. See
  [Create and Export in Bulk](/products/external-assessments/unsecured-emails/create-and-export-in-bulk).
</Note>
