---
title: Start a New Risk Assessment
description: 'Create a risk assessment and choose the options that shape the scan'
---

A Risk Assessment covers everything an External Assessment does, then adds internal
vulnerability analysis, sensitive data identification, browser credential analysis, and
ongoing risk monitoring. Setup takes a few minutes; the scan itself typically runs for
about 24 hours once agents are deployed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment picker">
    Click **Create Assessment** in the top right corner of the portal, then choose
    **Risk Assessment**. Each card lists its setup time, typical scan duration, and what
    the scan includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the company details">
    **Company Name** and **Domain** are required. If the company has no domain of its own,
    tick **Skip domain for external scan** and Telivy records `nodomain.local` instead,
    which runs the assessment without the external portion.

    You also set **Country**, **Company Status** (prospect or client), and the
    **Anti Virus** the company runs, so the agent guidance matches their environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule recurring scans (clients only)">
    When **Company Status** is set to client, a **Recurring Scans** section appears.
    Tick **Schedule Recurring Scans** and pick a **Scan Frequency** to keep the assessment
    refreshed automatically. See [Risk Monitoring](/products/risk-monitoring/overview) for
    what changes once monitoring is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide how PII is handled">
    Under **Configure Compliance**, **Mask PII data** stores only masked evidence of the
    sensitive data Telivy finds. Partners working with HIPAA-regulated clients usually
    want this on. See [Data Security](/products/risk-assessments/data-security) for what
    the platform stores either way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and configure">
    Click **Create** to land on the assessment's configuration page, where you deploy the
    agent and connect Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Whitelist the Telivy agent in the client's AV or EDR before deploying it, or scans will
  be blocked or quarantined. See
  [Whitelist Telivy with AV/EDR](/products/risk-assessments/whitelist-telivy-with-av-edr).
</Warning>
