---
title: "Understanding AI Scoring"
description: "A guide to how Telivy grades AI usage"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the AI Exposure Score Calculated?">
    The score is based on two factors detected across the assessed environment:

    - Tool Usage Volume: how many distinct AI applications are in use. Detecting any AI tool
      starts the count, and more than two, then more than five, each raise the exposure.
    - Adoption Rate: the share of users using AI tools. Adoption above 5%, 25%, and 50% of
      users each raise the exposure.

    Tool Usage Volume carries the heavier weight of the two. The combined result is
    normalized to a 0-100 scale, where a higher score means greater AI exposure.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How to Interpret the Score">
    Once calculated, the AI Exposure Score is normalized (0-100 scale) and classified into four risk levels:

    | Score Range | Risk Level | Meaning |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **0-20** | **Low (Green)** | Minimal AI risk exposure. |
    | **21-50** | **Moderate (Yellow)** | Some AI risk, but manageable with improvements. |
    | **51-80** | **High (Orange)** | Significant AI exposure, potential security risks. |
    | **81-100** | **Critical (Red)** | Major AI security gaps, immediate action required. |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
